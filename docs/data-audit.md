## Decision

**Median borrows/student is 1 (1–2 range, or otherwise below 3).** Collaborative boost would be noise on this little signal. Build Phases 2-3 only (content-based); keep Phase 4 as documented future work rather than building it against data too sparse to trust.

Subject/classification coverage is 0.0% (< 50%) — Phase 2's feature blob gives subject a ×2 weight, but that weight contributes nothing for a book with no subject on record. If this stays near 0%, the feature blob is effectively title + author + description; say so in the thesis rather than claiming subject-weighted matching that isn't really happening.

99.5% of the catalog has never been borrowed (> 80%) — expected for a school library, and it's the actual justification for content-based being the primary recommender rather than a nice-to-have.
